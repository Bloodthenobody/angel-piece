ANGEL PIECE ENDGAME

Rebirth

Reach the limits of your current journey, enter the Rebirth system and return stronger than before.

THE SYSTEM

How Rebirth Works

Rebirth begins the next chapter of your adventure.

♻️ Rebirth is an endgame progression system.

When you reach the required progression, you can reset part of your character's progress in exchange for permanent bonuses and access to stronger content.
1️⃣

Reach the Requirement

Become powerful enough to unlock your first Rebirth.

2️⃣

Enter the Temple

Visit the Rebirth Temple in the Celestial Realm.

3️⃣

Choose Rebirth

Confirm your decision and begin a new progression cycle.

PROGRESSION

What Changes?

Feature After Rebirth
Level Returns to the starting progression
Rebirth Count +1 permanently
Rebirth Power Increases
Permanent Unlocks Remain unlocked
Species Kept unless changed manually
Pets Kept
Discovered Areas Kept
⚡ The purpose of Rebirth is not simply to reset your character. It allows you to build a stronger character over multiple cycles.
