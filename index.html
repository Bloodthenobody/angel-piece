The Game

Angel Piece is an adventure game where players explore five seas, discover new islands, collect powerful items, fight enemies and bosses, and develop their character.

Fruits

Discover Angel Fruits and Demon Fruits with different powers and abilities.

Weapons

Find swords, guns and other powerful equipment.

Pets

Collect animals, Angel pets and Demon pets.

Bosses

Challenge powerful bosses throughout the five seas.

Abilities

Learn special abilities and develop your power.

Rebirth

Reach the required progression and discover the Celestial Realm.

The Five Seas

Sea 1

The beginning of the Angel Piece adventure. Explore the first islands and learn the game.

Sea 2

A stronger sea with new enemies, islands and challenges.

Sea 3

Discover stronger enemies, powerful items and new areas.

Sea 4

A dangerous sea containing powerful enemies and bosses.

Sea 5

The heavenly final sea and the beginning of the Celestial Realm journey.

Celestial Realm

A special area connected to the Rebirth system.

Choose Your Path

Angel Path

Follow the Angel path, help others and use your powers for good. Discover Angel species, Angel pets and powerful Angel Fruits.

Demon Path

Follow the Demon path and unlock special Demon powers. Demon players can discover Demon species, Demon pets and Demon Fruits.

Community

Angel Piece is built to grow with its players. Players can share ideas, report bugs and suggest new features for the game.
