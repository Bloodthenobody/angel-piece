PET SYSTEM

Build Your Pet Collection

Every pet has its own rarity, bonus and special ability.

🐾 Pets can help your adventure.

Pets can provide bonuses such as movement speed, damage, defense, healing or other special effects.

There are three main pet groups:

🐾 Neutral Pets — available to both factions.
😇 Angel Pets — connected to the Angel faction.
😈 Demon Pets — connected to the Demon faction.

SPECIES REWARD

50% Free Pet Chance

Changing your species can give you a special companion.

😇 Angel Species

50%

When an Angel player receives a new species, they have a 50% chance to receive a matching Angel Pet for free.

Example: Angelic Wolf → Celestial Wolf

😈 Demon Species

50%

When a Demon player receives a new species, they have a 50% chance to receive a matching Demon Pet for free.

Example: Demonic Dragon → Infernal Dragon

PET RULES

How Pets Work

🐾 Neutral Pets
Can be used by both Angels and Demons.

😇 Angel Pets
Can normally be used by Angel players.

😈 Demon Pets
Can normally be used by Demon players.

🎁 Species Reward
Changing Species costs 10 Cappies and gives a 50% chance to receive a matching faction pet for free.

⭐ Pet Growth
Pets can become stronger as their level increases.
