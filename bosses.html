BOSS SYSTEM

Prepare for Battle

The strongest enemies require teamwork, powerful Angel Fruits and skilled combat.

👑 Boss Battles

Bosses are much stronger than normal enemies.

⭐ Defeating a boss gives large amounts of XP and Cappies.

🎁 Some bosses can drop unique weapons, equipment and other rare items.

🌊 Some bosses can appear only in specific Seas or during special events.

⚔️ Mythical bosses are intended to be among the hardest fights in Angel Piece.
