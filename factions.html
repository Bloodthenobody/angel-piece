LIGHT FACTION

Angels

Angels are guardians of the heavenly realms. They focus on light, protection, speed and support abilities.

🐺 Angelic Wolf

A fast celestial species built around movement and close combat.

🐺 Pet: Celestial Wolf

🪽 Celestial Wing

A winged species with excellent aerial mobility.

🐦 Pet: Divine Bird

🦌 Holy Deer

A defensive species with healing and regeneration abilities.

🦌 Pet: Light Deer

🐉 Divine Dragon

A powerful celestial species with high defense and damage.

🐉 Pet: Divine Dragon

✨ Lightborn

A rare species connected directly to Angel energy.

✨ Pet: Light Spirit

🐺 Pet: Shadow Wolf

🦇 Pet: Infernal Bat

FACTION SYSTEM

Build Your Faction Rank

Complete quests and participate in faction battles to increase your rank.

Rank I Newcomer
Rank II Warrior
Rank III Elite
Rank IV Champion
⚔️ Faction PvP

Angels and Demons can fight each other in designated PvP areas.

Winning faction battles can reward Faction XP and Cappies.

Your faction rank can unlock new abilities, equipment and special quests.
SPECIES DOCTOR

Change Your Species

Visit the Species Doctor when you want to change your current species.

🏥 Species Doctor

The Species Doctor can change your current species to another species available to your faction.

💰 Every species change costs:
10 Cappies
🎁 Free Pet Chance

When you receive a new species, you have a:

50% chance to receive a free pet associated with your new species.

Example:
😇 Angelic Wolf → 🎁 Celestial Wolf Pet

😈 Demonic Dragon → 🎁 Infernal Dragon Pet
