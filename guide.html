WELCOME TO ANGEL PIECE

Game Guide

Everything you need to know before starting your journey across the five Seas.

START HERE

How to Begin

💰

Cappies

Cappies are the main currency. Use them to buy weapons, Angel Fruits, pets and other items.

⚔️

Choose Your Gear

Start by choosing your preferred combat style, weapon or Angel Fruit.

🗺️

Explore

Travel between islands, complete quests and discover new areas.

YOUR JOURNEY

Progression

1

Complete Quests

Defeat enemies and complete quests to gain experience and Cappies.

2

Upgrade Your Character

Improve your combat abilities, weapons, Angel Fruits and Species.

3

Explore New Islands

Reach the required levels to access stronger islands and Seas.

4

Fight Bosses

Powerful bosses provide better rewards and rare items.

5

Enter the Celestial Realm

Reach the highest progression and begin your Rebirth journey.

CORE SYSTEMS

Important Systems

🍎

Angel Fruits

Special powers with rarities, awakenings and advanced Ascensions.

😇

Species

Choose between Angel and Demon Species with different passive bonuses.

⚡

Abilities

Use combat abilities, Haki and special skills to become stronger.

🐾

Pets

Collect pets that can provide bonuses and special effects.

🚢

Boats

Use boats to travel between islands and encounter creatures at sea.

♻️

Rebirth

Reset your progression cycle in exchange for permanent power.

💡 Beginner Tip: Don't spend all your Cappies immediately. Upgrade your combat setup first and save enough currency for important upgrades and travel.
🌊 Explorer Tip: Not every secret location is visible from the main path. Explore islands carefully.
👑 Boss Tip: If a boss is too strong, return later with better equipment, abilities or a stronger Species.
