SPECIES SYSTEM

How Species Work

😇 Angel Species focus more on speed, energy, defense and celestial abilities.

😈 Demon Species focus more on strength, damage, durability and destructive abilities.

🐾 Species can also interact with certain pets and abilities to create different builds.
LUCK

Species & Pet Bonus

🎁 Some Species have a 50% chance to receive a free Species-linked pet when obtaining that Species for the first time.

For example, certain Angel Species can receive an Angel pet, while certain Demon Species can receive a Demon pet.
